WHAT TO DRAW
A character sprite sheet in the exact same pixel art style, pixel scale, palette and outline colour as the attached village, on a perfectly flat, solid, pure magenta #FF00FF background (no shadow, no gradient, no ground, nothing else on the background).
Top row, four characters evenly spaced with lots of magenta space between them, each about 40 art pixels tall, front-facing, full body:
(1) the player: a young courier with a short red-orange scarf, a leather satchel, cream shirt, brown boots;
(2) the scribe agent: round-faced, big round glasses, terracotta-orange hooded tunic, holding a quill;
(3) the tinkerer agent: small, brass goggles on the forehead, slate grey-blue apron with tools, holding a tiny wrench;
(4) the cartographer agent: moss-green hooded cloak, holding a rolled map and a small lantern.
Bottom row, the same three agents (not the player) in an active "working" pose, aligned under their idle versions: the scribe writing in the air with small sparkles, the tinkerer hammering with sparks, the cartographer unrolling a map.
Characters never touch or overlap each other. No text, labels, numbers, logos or watermark.
