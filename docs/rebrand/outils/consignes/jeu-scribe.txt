WHAT TO DRAW
The animation sprite sheet of one inhabitant of the attached game: THE SCRIBE, a small round-faced character with brown hair, big round glasses (two simple rings around the dot eyes) and a terracotta-orange tunic whose terracotta HOOD is always up over the head, a cream collar, a brown belt, brown boots. Chibi proportions, always seen from the front (facing the camera). Every standing figure has exactly the same size: about 30 art pixels tall and 20 art pixels wide (about 180 image pixels tall).
Layout: a grid of 4 rows and 4 columns, evenly spaced, each figure centred in its cell, the feet of each row on the same baseline.
Row 1: (a) idle, standing, holding a feather quill; (b) idle, the same pose, eyes closed for a blink; (c) waiting: holding up a large unrolled cream parchment with both hands toward the viewer; (d) waiting: the same, parchment raised a little higher, surprised face.
Row 2, working: writing with a big feather quill on a sheet of paper floating in front of the chest, four successive frames of the writing motion (quill up, quill down, quill to the right, quill back), two tiny sparkles near the quill.
Row 3: (a) sleeping, sitting on the ground, head bowed, eyes closed, hands on the knees; (b) the same, head a bit lower. Do not draw any Z letters. The last two cells stay empty.
Row 4: (a) talking: one hand raised in a friendly gesture, mouth open; (b) cheering: both arms up, happy. The last two cells stay empty.
Same character, same colours, same proportions and same size in every frame; only the arms, the head and the props move.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, grid lines or watermark.
