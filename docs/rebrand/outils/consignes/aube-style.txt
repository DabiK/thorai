STYLE
Original true pixel art for a top-down 2D exploration RPG, in bright morning light. Crisp square pixels: every art pixel is a solid block of exactly 3x3 image pixels (the artwork is about 512 x 341 art pixels, upscaled 3 times with nearest neighbour). No anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photo texture. Limited palette of about 24 colours: fresh spring greens, turquoise water, warm cream paths, terracotta and coral roofs, a few saturated accents. Dark coloured outlines (deep blue-green, never pure black) around characters and props. Tiles on a 16x16 art-pixel grid. Camera: classic top-down RPG oblique view (we see the ground from above and the front walls of buildings). Cheerful, airy, lots of breathing room, flowers, small details.
Entirely original design: do not imitate any existing game's characters, creatures, tiles, buildings or interface (not Pokemon, not Zelda, not Stardew Valley, not Final Fantasy, not Earthbound).
