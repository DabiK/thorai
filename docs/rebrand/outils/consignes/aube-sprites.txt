STYLE
Original true pixel art sprites for a top-down 2D exploration RPG in bright morning light, with exactly the rendering of the attached hamlet: same palette, same dark blue-green outlines, same soft three-tone shading with the light coming from the top left. Crisp square pixels: every art pixel is a solid block of exactly @@PX@@x@@PX@@ image pixels, aligned on a @@PX@@-pixel grid. No anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no dithering noise, no painterly brushwork, no photo texture. Limited palette of about 24 colours: fresh greens, warm creams, terracotta, coral, slate blue, browns, brass yellow. A one-art-pixel dark outline (deep blue-green, never pure black) around every character and object. Camera: classic top-down RPG oblique view (we see the ground from above and the front of things). Characters are chibi (big head, small body).
BACKGROUND: a perfectly flat, solid, pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere around the sprites: no ground, no cast shadow, no gradient, no texture, no frame, no grid lines. Sprites never touch each other nor the image border; leave wide magenta gaps between them.
Entirely original design: do not imitate any existing game's characters, creatures, tiles, buildings or interface (not Pokemon, not Zelda, not Stardew Valley, not Final Fantasy, not Earthbound).
