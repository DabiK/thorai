WHAT TO DRAW
The animation sprite sheet of one inhabitant of the attached game: THE TINKERER, a small cheerful character with short curly blond hair, brass goggles pushed up on the forehead, a slate grey-blue apron with a few tools over a cream shirt, brown boots. Chibi proportions, always seen from the front (facing the camera). Every standing figure has exactly the same size: about 30 art pixels tall and 20 art pixels wide (about 180 image pixels tall).
Layout: a grid of 4 rows and 4 columns, evenly spaced, each figure centred in its cell, the feet of each row on the same baseline.
Row 1: (a) idle, standing, holding a small wrench; (b) idle, the same pose, eyes closed for a blink; (c) waiting: holding up a large unrolled cream parchment with both hands toward the viewer; (d) waiting: the same, parchment raised a little higher, surprised face.
Row 2, working: hammering on a tiny anvil standing in front of the feet, four successive frames (hammer raised high, hammer coming down, hammer hitting with small yellow sparks, hammer rebounding).
Row 3: (a) sleeping, sitting on the ground, head bowed, goggles over the eyes, hands on the knees; (b) the same, head a bit lower. Do not draw any Z letters. The last two cells stay empty.
Row 4: (a) talking: one hand raised in a friendly gesture, mouth open; (b) cheering: both arms up, happy. The last two cells stay empty.
Same character, same colours, same proportions and same size in every frame; only the arms, the head and the props move.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, grid lines or watermark.
